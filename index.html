Should I text this person while intoxicated? Let the panel decide.

  1. You start out at a bar (simulated GPS). Tap the location card to change it.

  3. Pass the face scan. Your selfie and text go to your three-judge panel.
  4. Thumbs up, it sends. Thumbs down, it dies.
